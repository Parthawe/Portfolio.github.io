# Project intro evidence ledger

Internal editorial record for the 33 recruiter-facing project openings. The canonical public copy is `Project.pageIntro` in `src/data/projects.ts`; this ledger records why each proof is safe to show.

Evidence kinds are deliberately distinct: **measured** is a documented before/after, **observed** is a research or event observation, **shipped** is a released artifact, **artifact** is inspectable built work, **target** is an explicitly labelled design goal, and **scope** describes the work without implying impact. “NDA-safe” means the intro contains no private client metric or implementation detail.

## Editorial exclusions

- Do not publish Mentra’s ambiguous 88%, Omakase’s unverified 200+ plays, Drowning’s unsupported 100+ audience, or Ballah’s former 17-tool claim.
- Do not describe Office of Diversity as WCAG-certified.
- Do not turn targets, prototypes, concepts, team research counts, or artifact scope into achieved user impact.
- TransFi, ZentiPay, CueTV, and AI Voice remain qualitative and public-safe until stronger publishable evidence is supplied.
