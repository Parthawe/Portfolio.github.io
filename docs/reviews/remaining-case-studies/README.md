## Sources and limits

- All product screenshots, films and original demonstrations retain their provenance. No paid service, runtime generation service or new animation framework was used.
- Anatomy’s existing MIT-licensed kit generates the Code for Build block assemblies. These are an explanatory lesson based on the original block grammar, not recovered app code or a validated learning experiment.
- The three scenic studies are proportional reconstructions from the original local photographs and drawings. They omit undocumented construction. Lighting is illustrative. They remain drafts, without standalone public experience links or approval changes.
- Sculpture does not have sufficient multi-angle coverage for a faithful full object. Its source folder also contains unrelated knife/plate inspiration images; those were excluded from the sculpture inspection.
- The Making of Time asset named `watch-components.png` depicts the finished watch. It does not establish a movement layout or gear ratios. No unseen movement was invented.
- [Sakshi Oswal’s author record](https://in.linkedin.com/in/sakshi-oswal) and [Saurabh Rane’s author record](https://in.linkedin.com/in/saurabhcrane) list *Privacy Preserving Cancer Prognosis via Fully Homomorphic Encryption* as an IP.com defensive publication dated May 11, 2022. The original IP.com document was not recovered. The website links an author record and does not label it a paper download or peer-reviewed clinical evidence.
- [TED’s November 2019 event record](https://www.ted.com/talks/7_perspectives_towards_a_better_mental_health) is an event reference. Its footage has not been established as a full recording of the photographed stage production.
- Mentra brand improvements use existing original renders. No AI-generated product image was needed.

## Private delivery

Detailed NYU research, CueTV screens and related derivatives were moved to the ignored local `private-nda/refinement` directory. Their former source pages are preserved there too. They are not imported by the public application.

No private delivery service is configured. The two public pages therefore offer a private-review request, without a reviewer-code field that pretends to retrieve unavailable content. Previously deployed material is outside this local-only change; no live files or deployment were modified.

## Performance

The homepage comparison uses local production previews at 1440 × 1000, cold HTTP cache, 5 Mbps, 80 ms latency and 4× CPU throttling. The hero renderer, lighting data and still composition are unchanged.

| Quieter, alternating-order comparison | Before | After |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Median hero readiness | 5,020 ms | 5,078 ms |
| Mean hero readiness | 5,028 ms | 5,071 ms |
| Median first contentful paint | 908 ms | 880 ms |
| Resource body bytes through readiness | ~1.90 MB | ~1.90 MB |
| Median startup layout shift | 0.0208 | 0.0208 |
| Shader programs | 20 | 20 |

Three paired samples do not establish field percentiles or a speed improvement. The median readiness difference is approximately 58 ms, or 1.2%. The identical-build control and initial five-pair batch are retained alongside the quieter retest, rather than discarded. The initial batch had substantial variability: before 5.06–10.11s, after 5.25–9.86s. An earlier functional first-frame check also timed out during overlapping build/QA work; its isolated rerun passed.

[Summary](performance-summary.json) · [Initial five-pair samples](performance-samples.json) · [Identical-build control](performance-aa-samples.json) · [Alternating-order retest](performance-balanced-samples.json)

The optional scenic studies created zero study canvases before activation and one while open. Measured first draw after activation was 632 ms for The Dumb Waiter, 469 ms for Drowning and 384 ms for TEDx, on an unthrottled local Chromium run after the page had loaded. Activation transfers included shared dependencies, approximately 212 KB per cold context. All three recorded **zero additional draws during a 500 ms idle observation** after settling; closing each removed its study canvas and disposed the renderer. These are local activation measurements, not full-page startup or device guarantees. [Raw scenic measurements](scenic-cost.json).

Stage lettering is isolated from the homepage’s Three.js chunk. The free Helvetiker font’s distribution notice is retained in `public/Assets/licenses/helvetiker.txt`. No new model textures, runtime generation service or WebGL context was added to the homepage/footer.
