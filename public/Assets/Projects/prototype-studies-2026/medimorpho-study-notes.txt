Rehearse communication across a visit

A provisional, nonclinical browser concept informed by the project’s language-and-navigation research topic. Prepare a sample communication card, make a staff/interpreter handoff explicit and check next-step understanding.

Evidence
The public research framing covers language and navigation before, during and after a healthcare visit. Original research, findings and participant material remain reserved for private review.

Limits
This is not the hospital’s patient portal or a validated care service. It gives no medical advice, clinical translation, appointment booking or care plan, and uses no patient records. Interpreter availability is simulated.

Proposed evaluation
Review with appropriate clinical, language-access and accessibility professionals before any patient-facing use. Test comprehension and handoff continuity without inventing participant outcomes or clinical benefit.

This is a newly implemented browser adaptation. Original Figma files are unchanged. No new interviews, comparative outcomes or operational benefit are claimed.
