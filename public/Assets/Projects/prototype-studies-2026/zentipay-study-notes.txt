Review the token transfer before confirming

A new working adaptation of the original centred token-transfer screen. The recipient, token, balance and invoice remain connected through review, a simulated settlement and a persistent transfer record.

Evidence
Original send-crypto screen, completed-transfer invoice, wallet overview and transaction history from the project archive.

Limits
USDT amounts use fixed two-decimal fixtures, not token-network precision or a live exchange quote. Contacts are fictional. No wallet, chain, email, identity or payment provider is connected.

Proposed evaluation
Ask users to explain the token, recipient and total debit before confirmation. Test a failed settlement and retrieval of the correct receipt; do not infer financial trust or transfer performance from software checks.

This is a newly implemented browser adaptation. Original Figma files are unchanged. No new interviews, comparative outcomes or operational benefit are claimed.
