The Omakase

2-player party arcade game where sushi chefs compete to serve customers

Year2024
RoleCreator
Contents Gameplay Fabrication Exhibition

Overview

Serve or Be Served

The Omakase is a 2-player party arcade game. As two sushi chefs, you compete against each other by serving different customers their feast. The arcade has two sets of controls, each containing 8 buttons that change color throughout gameplay. You need to serve quick and accurate — though you will get messy.

Play at vill4n3lle.itch.io/the-omakase →

01 — Gameplay

Gameplay

Two players face off as competing sushi chefs. Each player has 8 color-changing buttons mapped to ingredients. Orders appear on screen and players must match the correct sequence before their opponent.

02 — Fabrication

Fabrication

Custom arcade cabinet built with laser-cut panels, Arduino Mega controllers, and 16 individually addressable RGB buttons. The cabinet design references classic Japanese arcade aesthetics.

03 — Exhibition

Exhibition

Exhibited at ITP Spring Show 2024. Players competed in live tournaments, with the game generating an energetic, competitive atmosphere.
