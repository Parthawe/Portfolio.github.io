Drowning

Scenic design for stage production inspired by abandoned greenhouse aesthetics

Year2024
RoleSet Designer
Contents Concept Design Production

Overview

Abandoned Greenhouse

The scenic design for Drowning draws deeply from the haunting beauty of an abandoned greenhouse. Layers of cracked glass and rusted metal frame an atmosphere both fragile and resilient, symbolizing themes of entrapment and decay. Overgrown plants spill from corners and crawl along walls, reclaiming space with quiet but unstoppable force.

01 — Concept

Concept

The set’s textures and muted colors create a visually rich but melancholic environment, echoing the isolation and vulnerability of the characters, suspended in a world forgotten yet teeming with echoes of life.

02 — Design

Design

Layers of translucent and opaque materials create depth on stage. Practical lighting filters through cracked glass panels, casting fractured shadows. Live plants integrated into the set evolve subtly over the run of the production.

03 — Production

Production

Collaboration with director and lighting designer to create atmospheric shifts through the play. The greenhouse structure allowed for dynamic lighting states, from harsh daylight to intimate twilight.
