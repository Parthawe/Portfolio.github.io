# Project quality review — 5 October 2026

Reviewed 49 project routes in the local production build at commit 0173cdd. Scores judge the public portfolio case study: clarity, original evidence, design reasoning, presentation, and credibility. They are editorial judgments, not user-test results. Protected work is scored on its public preview. Source inspection covered full stories; browser inspection covered route entry and selected expanded sections. This is not an exhaustive mobile, accessibility, or performance audit.

## Design specificity

The strongest work feels authored: Jugalbandi's pneumatic/acoustic constraints, Mentra Brand's factory-ready packaging, and Omakase's custom controls give the stories substance. Shared typography and reading rails are coherent. Repeated abstract language about trust and clarity, feature inventories, and course-note covers make the weaker cases less distinct. Reconstructed demos and unavailable original artifacts are generally labeled honestly.

## Priority issues

1. **P1 — Incomplete archives dilute the project collection.** Production Studio, Applications, Hypercinema, Embodied Web, Performance by Design, Feeling Patterns, Storytelling, and Messy Humans lack essential original artifacts. Group them into a course archive or recover recordings, builds, drafts, and research records. Suggested action: $impeccable shape.
2. **P1 — Outcome claims need provenance.** Mentra's improvements need dates and sample sizes; Omakase's audience count needs an observation basis; The Point's phone-only figure needs a source; MONIAC's learning claim needs evidence or narrower language. Suggested action: $impeccable clarify.
3. **P2 — Final screens often crowd out design reasoning.** Show one rejected alternative, the constraint that ruled it out, and the revision that followed. Trim repeated premises and reflections. Suggested action: $impeccable distill.
4. **P2 — Motion and media need another technical pass.** Black Hole has an autoplay loop without controls. Shared lazy images often omit intrinsic dimensions, creating a layout-shift risk. Several stills exceed 1 MB without responsive sources. These are source findings, not measured battery or CLS results. Suggested action: $impeccable harden, then $impeccable optimize.
5. **P2 — Factual and metadata checks remain.** Review Black Hole's scientific wording with its mathematical collaborator and clarify VishwaConclave's three-year story versus its date range. Suggested action: $impeccable clarify.

## Reader perspectives

- **Recruiter scanning quickly:** 49 equally framed pages make it difficult to distinguish shipped work, concepts, course notes, and personal essays.
- **Design lead evaluating judgment:** Many cases show polished final output but omit a rejected route or a decision changed by research.
- **Reader sensitive to motion or on a slower connection:** Black Hole's uncontrolled loop and full-resolution in-page images warrant correction.
