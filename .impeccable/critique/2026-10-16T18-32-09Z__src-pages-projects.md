## Design assessment

The editorial identity and original artifacts feel authored for this portfolio. The generic parts are repeated explanations, generated covers that hide actual work, and archive reflections given the weight of full case studies. The main opportunity is one original artifact, one concrete design choice, and its demonstrated consequence early in each case. Original films, fabricated mechanisms, font files, source screens and production specifications are the strongest material.
