Moniac Machine

Economic strategy game inspired by the legendary 1949 Phillips hydraulic computer

Year2024
RoleCreator
Contents Inspiration Mechanics Result

Overview

Master the Economy

Dive into the world of Moniac Game, where you master the ebb and flow of a nation’s fortunes. Drawing from the legendary 1949 Phillips Moniac Machine, you juggle fiscal levers and monetary policy while navigating financial crises. Each twist of a tax knob and allocation of government gold could either lift your economy to dizzying heights or plunge it into despair.

01 — Inspiration

Inspiration

The original MONIAC (Monetary National Income Analogue Computer) was a hydraulic machine built in 1949 by economist Bill Phillips. It used water flowing through pipes to model economic systems. This game translates that physical metaphor into a digital interactive experience.

02 — Mechanics

Mechanics

Players adjust tax rates, government spending, and interest rates through physical sliders and knobs. The economy responds in real-time with visual feedback showing GDP, inflation, and employment flowing through the system.

03 — Result

Result

The game creates an intuitive understanding of macroeconomic feedback loops that textbooks struggle to convey.
